## Summary of origin chat

The project aims to solve cross-border returns for exporters selling to US customers. The proposed idea is to offer return consolidation at a domestic US address rather than managing individual reverse logistics per parcel. This service targets export brands whose current alternative is simply to eat the loss when a US customer returns an item. For this model to succeed, the underlying assumptions must hold: nobody currently solves cross-border returns for exporters, and individual international return shipping costs more than the product itself. The founder reached the conclusion that establishing a US consolidation address serves as the strategic entry wedge over traditional per-parcel reverse logistics.
