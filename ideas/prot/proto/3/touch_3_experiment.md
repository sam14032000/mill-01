## Stop / Scale Decision Rule

* **STOP SEQUENCE (Touch Cap Reached):** If Opt-out/Negative replies exceed 2.5% across 50 sends, immediately cap cold sequences at 2 touches.
* **PROCEED TO TOUCH 4:** If Unsubscribe stays < 1.5% and Positive Reply Rate > 3.0%, validate Touch 4 breakup email.
